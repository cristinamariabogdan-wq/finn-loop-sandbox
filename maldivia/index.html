Care

Arrive to a home,
not a list of jobs.

Our on-site team looks after your home throughout the year, whether you are here or away. You tell us when you are coming. Everything else is already done.

  • 01

    Before you land

    The house opened, cooled, provisioned to your list, and the boat fuelled and waiting at your mooring.

  • 02

    While you are here

    Housekeeping, kitchen, dive and boat crew — present when you want them, invisible when you do not.

  • 03

    While you are away

    Structural, mechanical and marine maintenance on a fixed calendar, with a written report each quarter.
