# And'a Pizza

register: brand

## Product Purpose
One-page site for And'a Pizza, a Romanian mobile wood-fired pizza catering business. Anda brings a 500°C stone oven to your event and bakes live. The page's single job: make visitors crave the pizza and send a WhatsApp booking inquiry.

## Users
People in Romania planning private events (birthdays, weddings, garden parties, corporate). Browsing on phones, often evenings, often referred from Instagram/Facebook. They decide with their eyes and stomach; the booking is a low-commitment WhatsApp message, not a payment.

## Brand & Tone
Warm, artisanal, personal ("Anda" is a real person). Fire, char, flour, 24h cold-fermented dough. Romanian language throughout. Confident but homemade, not corporate chain pizza.

## Anti-references
Dominos/pizza-chain aesthetics, red-green Italian flag clichés, sterile SaaS landing pages, cold minimal white space that starves the appetite.

## Strategic principles
- Appetite first: photography is the argument; copy supports it.
- Zero-friction ordering: pick quantities inline, one WhatsApp tap.
- Trust through process: live baking, dough story, real numbers.
